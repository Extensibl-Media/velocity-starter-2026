## Why seasonal maintenance matters

A little upkeep goes a long way. Catching a lifted shingle or a clogged gutter early is the difference between a five-minute fix and a five-figure repair.

### Spring

- Clear gutters and downspouts of winter debris
- Look for shingles loosened by freeze-thaw cycles
- Check flashing around chimneys and vents

### Summer

- Inspect the attic for signs of heat damage or poor ventilation
- Trim overhanging branches before storm season

### Fall

- Clean gutters again ahead of the leaves
- Book a professional inspection before winter

### Winter

- Watch for ice dams along the eaves
- Keep an eye on the ceiling for new water stains

When in doubt, call a licensed roofer. A quick professional inspection is inexpensive insurance against a major repair.
