## First, stay safe

After a big storm, don't climb onto the roof yourself. Assess from the ground and call a professional for anything more than a quick look.

### Step 1: Document everything

Take dated photos of any visible damage — missing shingles, dented gutters, debris. This is the backbone of a smooth insurance claim.

### Step 2: Prevent further damage

If water is getting in, a temporary tarp can limit interior damage. Keep receipts for any emergency work.

### Step 3: Call a licensed roofer

A reputable roofer will inspect for hail bruising and wind lift that isn't obvious from the ground, and can meet your adjuster on site.

### Step 4: File your claim promptly

Most policies have a filing window. Don't wait — start the claim while the storm date is fresh and documented.
