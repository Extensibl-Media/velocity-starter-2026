## Repair or replace?

Every roof reaches the end of its life eventually. Here are five signs it's time to stop patching and start planning a replacement.

1. **Your roof is 20+ years old.** Most asphalt shingle roofs last 20–25 years. If yours is in that range, budget for a replacement.
2. **Shingles are curling or missing.** Widespread curling, cracking, or bald spots mean the shingles have lost their protective granules.
3. **You see daylight in the attic.** Light coming through the roof boards means water can get in too.
4. **Sagging rooflines.** A sagging deck points to trapped moisture and possible structural issues.
5. **Granules in the gutters.** A little is normal after installation; a lot signals advanced wear.

If you're seeing two or more of these, schedule a professional inspection. We'll give you an honest assessment — repair when we can, replace only when you should.
